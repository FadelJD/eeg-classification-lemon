# Methods

One paragraph per pipeline step, with the `config.yaml` keys that govern it and the function that implements it. Values marked GUESS are not specified by the paper; the full list is in `notes/handoff.md` and `notes/DECISIONS.md`.

**Data.** MPI-Leipzig LEMON preprocessed resting-state EEG stands in for the paper's unavailable dataset. The files are EEGLAB `.set`/`.fdt`, already downsampled to 250 Hz, band-pass filtered and ICA-cleaned by the LEMON authors. We use the eyes-closed recording (`signal.condition: EC`, GUESS), which concatenates eight 1-minute blocks. Sex and 5-year age bins come from `Participants_MPILMBB_LEMON.csv` (`participants.*`, `age_midpoints`). 203 subjects have EEG. Two recorded at 100 Hz are excluded (`cache/skipped.csv`), leaving 201: 73 female, 128 male. Reading and labels: `pipeline.features.read_raw`, `load_labels`.

**Channels.** The paper's 19 channels (`signal.channels`, order fixed) are picked case-insensitively. In 70 subjects LEMON had removed one to four of them as bad; these are rebuilt by spherical-spline interpolation (`signal.missing_channel_policy: interpolate`, GUESS) from the remaining ~60 channels. The reference is kept as delivered (`signal.reference: keep`, GUESS). Implementation: `pipeline.features.pick_channels`, `_interpolate_missing`.

**Epochs and sections.** The first 5 s are discarded (`signal.discard_start_s`, paper §3.2). The rest is cut into non-overlapping 2-s epochs (`signal.epoch_s`, paper) and grouped into consecutive 30-s sections of 15 epochs (`sections.section_epochs`, paper). Each section is one sample. Females contribute 8 sections and males 5 (`sections.per_class`), which reverses the paper's 5/8 because LEMON's imbalance runs the other way. Validation-style "long" rows use one 3-minute block of 90 epochs per subject (`sections.long_epochs`, paper §3.5; `long_start_epoch: 0`, GUESS). Implementation: `pipeline.features.epoch_data`, `_extract`.

**Connectivity features.** For every channel pair, magnitude coherence |E[Sxy]| / √(E[Sxx]·E[Syy]) is computed across the epochs of a section (`connectivity.method: coh`, paper Eq. 1). The estimator is `mne_connectivity.spectral_connectivity_epochs` with multitaper spectra (`connectivity.mode`, GUESS). Coherence is averaged over the frequency bins of five bands, delta 0–4, theta 4–8, alpha 8–13, beta 13–30 and gamma 30–45 Hz (`connectivity.bands`, paper §3.3; boundary-bin assignment `band_edges`, GUESS). That gives 171 pairs (`np.triu_indices(19, 1)`) × 5 bands = 855 features in band-major order. Implementation: `pipeline.features.connectivity_vector`. Per-subject results are cached under a hash of these keys (`cache/subjects/<hash>/`), so section counts can be changed without recomputing.

**Feature selection.** An XGBoost classifier (`selection.xgb_params`, GUESS) is fit on the sections of a random 90 % of subjects (`selection.subject_frac`), and all 855 features are ranked by gain importance (`selection.importance_type`, GUESS). This is repeated 50 times (`selection.n_trials`). The 855 × 50 rank matrix is aggregated by first appearance: walking ranks from best to worst, a feature enters the list the first time it appears in any trial (`selection.aggregation`). The top 34 are kept (`selection.n_keep`). Implementation: `pipeline.selection.select_features`, `aggregate_first_appearance`. The mean-rank alternative is `aggregate_mean_rank`.

**Pooled vs in-fold selection.** *Pooled*: selection runs once on all subjects before cross-validation, as the paper does. The test subjects' labels influence which features are chosen, so pooled scores are optimistic. *In-fold*: selection runs on each training fold only, which is leak-free. Implementation: `pipeline.harness.evaluate(selection=...)`.

**Classifiers and validation.** Each model is a pipeline of `StandardScaler` followed by the classifier: XGB, MLP, RBF-SVM (Platt-calibrated), random forest, and logistic regression as a baseline (`models`, `model_params`, all GUESS). The paper's XGB/MLP/SVM/RF hyperparameters are not given. Cross-validation is `StratifiedGroupKFold` with subjects as groups: 10 splits (`cv.n_splits`) repeated over 5 seeds (`cv.seeds`). The harness asserts in every fold that no subject is on both sides. Test rows are either the subject's sections (`cv.test_representation: sections`) or its single 90-epoch row (`long`, the paper's validation scheme). Section-level metrics score each row. Subject-level metrics average a subject's predicted P(female) across its rows. Balanced accuracy uses a fixed threshold of 0.5 (`cv.threshold`); the paper tunes θ and reports 0.39. Each table entry is the mean ± sd over seeds of the per-seed fold mean. Implementation: `pipeline.harness.evaluate`, `pipeline.models.make_model`.

**Permutation test.** For the best pooled configuration, labels are shuffled between subjects (a subject's rows keep one label). Pooled selection and CV are rerun each time, on 1 seed (`permutation.*`), and p = (#{null ≥ observed} + 1)/(n + 1). Implementation: `pipeline.harness.permute_subject_labels`, `scripts/stage2.py permutation`.

**Extensions.** Age clusters split at bin midpoint 45 (young 20–40, older 55–80); the best in-fold configuration is trained and tested within each cluster (`scripts/stage2.py age_clusters`). Age confound: features are regressed on age bin midpoint inside each training fold (`cv.residualise_age`, `pipeline.confound.residualise`) before in-fold selection. Stability: mean pairwise Jaccard of the 50 in-fold 34-sets. Aggregation: overlap of the pooled first-appearance and mean-rank top 34. Sectioning ablations: the per-subject cache is reassembled with 5/5 and with the paper-literal 5/8 sections per female/male (`sections.per_class`) and rerun in-fold.
